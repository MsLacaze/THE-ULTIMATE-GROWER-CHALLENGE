Challenge 2 — Plant emergency room15 min

🚑 Diagnose the Garden

Good horticulturalists diagnose before they treat.

Case A: Lower leaves are yellowing and the soil stays wet for several days.
Investigate drainage/waterlogging and root condition.
Immediately add more water.
Assume insects caused it.
Case B: New shoots are distorted and covered in tiny insects.
Investigate aphids and inspect the plant.
Remove every insect in the garden.
Add fertiliser immediately.
Case C: Seeds were planted but very few germinated.
Investigate seed viability, depth, moisture, temperature and conditions.
Water constantly.
